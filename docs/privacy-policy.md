# Active40 privacy policy (draft)

Last updated: [DATE]

Active40 is a training journal for strength training, running and daily recovery check-ins. This policy describes what the app does with your information.

## Summary

- Everything you record stays on your phone.
- There is no account and no sign-in.
- The app has no internet connection. It does not send data anywhere.
- There are no ads, analytics or crash reporting services.

## What the app stores

The app stores the information you enter yourself:

- Strength sessions: exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, optional effort ratings and notes
- Runs: date, distance, duration, run type, optional effort and notes
- Daily check-ins: sleep, energy, soreness, optional notes
- Optional midlife symptoms (for example night sweats or joint aches), only if you turn on symptom tracking
- Your settings, such as units and whether symptom tracking is on

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, upload or share your data with us or anyone else.
- It does not request internet, location, contacts, camera or storage permissions.
- Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your data is not copied to a cloud backup by the system.

## Notifications and vibration

When you use the rest timer, the app can vibrate and, if you allow notifications, show a notification when the rest period ends. This happens on your phone only.

## Exporting and importing

You can export all your data to a file using Settings, Export data. You choose where the file is saved using the system file picker. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. You can import such a file later, for example on a new phone. The app itself never sends the file anywhere.

## Deleting your data

You can delete all your data at any time in Settings, Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes all data stored by the app on your phone. Exported files you have saved elsewhere are not affected and can be deleted by you.

## Medical disclaimer

Active40 is a training journal. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

## Children

Active40 is intended for adults.
